SizeScan Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Your documents stay on your device

Scans, photos and files you create are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We do not upload, collect or have access to them. Files leave your device only when you choose to share or save them.

Permissions

The app does not request camera or storage permissions. Scanning uses the Google Play services document scanner, and photos are picked with the Android photo picker. Only the items you select are read.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address (approximate location), app interaction data, and crash and performance diagnostics to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In regions where consent is required (e.g. EEA, UK), you are asked for consent first and can change it any time from the app menu ("Privacy settings"). See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google Play services

The document scanner and in-app review are provided by Google Play services, subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting data

Delete documents in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all data it stored.

Contact

Questions: use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play page.
