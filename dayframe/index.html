Dayframe

One question, one photo, every day.

Dayframe gives you a small daily question for your child, your pet, your plant or yourself. Answer with a line and a photo, and watch the days add up into weekly cards, a year in pixels and "on this day" look-backs.

Send the same question to someone you love. Their answer comes back into your day, with no accounts and no server.
