365 questions to ask your kids, one for every day

“How was school?” usually gets “fine.” A specific, slightly playful question gets a real answer, and a small window into how your child thinks.

Below is a question for every day of the year, grouped by month. Seasonal questions sit in the right season (Northern Hemisphere), and some are marked for ages 3–6 or 7–12. The rest work for any age.

How to use them

  • Ask one at dinner, in the car or at bedtime. One is enough.
  • Write the answer down word for word, with the date. Kids' exact words are the part you will want to remember.
  • Don't correct or steer the answer. Follow up with “why?” or “what happened next?”
  • Come back to the same question a year later and compare the answers.
