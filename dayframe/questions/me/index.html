365 daily journal prompts, one for every day

A blank page is the hardest part of journaling. A small question removes it: you only have to answer one thing, and a minute is enough.

Below is one prompt for every day of the year, grouped by month, with seasonal questions in the right season (Northern Hemisphere). They are gentle on purpose: no productivity homework, just noticing your day.

How to use them

  • Answer today's prompt in one or two lines. Short is fine; skipping a day is fine.
  • Add the date. Patterns only show up when you can look back.
  • If a prompt doesn't fit, answer the opposite: “nothing today” is also an answer.
  • Reread a month at a time, or the same date a year later.
